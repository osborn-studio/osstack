# Decisions

## D-001 Existing osstack Is The Base

osstack remains the primary architecture. External products inform evidence, governance, and distribution layers, but osstack keeps its loop runtime, daemon, router, kanban, and worktree isolation.

## D-002 Fail Open By Default

Knowledge Cloud, compliance rendering, proof extras, and governance warnings must not block the loop unless strict mode is requested.

## D-003 Evidence Must Be Real

Reports, proof packs, proof views, compliance mappings, and PR bodies must not synthesize absent evidence. Missing evidence is rendered as `unknown` or `MISSING`.

## D-004 Cloud Knowledge Must Be Osstack-Native

External material may be input, but published knowledge must use Osstack taxonomy, retain internal provenance, and pass retrieval evals.
