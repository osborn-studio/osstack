# Report Templates And Outputs

## Outputs

- `reports/osstack-report-<id>.md`
- `release/osstack-scorecard-<id>.md`
- `release/osstack-scorecard-<id>.html`
- `release/osstack-proof-<id>/`
- `release/osstack-proof-<id>.zip`
- `reports/pr-body-<id>.md`
- `reports/compliance-<pack>-<id>.md`

## Report Sections

- summary
- completed tasks
- policy decisions
- verify results
- governance findings
- knowledge trace
- worktree status
- events timeline
- risks and next steps
- conformance level

## Output Rules

- Missing optional evidence renders as `unknown` or `MISSING`.
- Reports must not invent policy decisions, reviews, or compliance evidence.
- Proof and compliance outputs must retain pack ids, versions, and checksums.
