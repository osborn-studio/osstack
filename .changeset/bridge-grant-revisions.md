Add signed live revision grants for the Grok bridge (phase 5-A, migration 53). `osstack mission grant-revisions --mission ROOT` (or `--chain CHAIN` to extend one) needs an interactive terminal on stdin and stderr, shows the exact grant the daemon previewed (repository, PR, head, base, scope, principal, rounds, expiry, budget), requires typing `GRANT <preview digest>`, and only then reads the host-private Ed25519 issuer key (`bridge/revision-issuer-<key>.pem`, mode 0600) and signs; the daemon only verifies the signature against `bridge/revision-issuer.json`, so a process that merely holds the RPC credential cannot grant. Defaults follow the owner decision: 1 round (`--rounds 2` explicit, 2 is the cap), valid 24 hours (at most 7 days), review deadline 24 hours reset after each confirmed push, budget 3600 s executor, 3600 s container, 48 h wall clock. The daemon re-reads the live PR before issuing (open, authored by the registered publisher, published head and base) and re-checks every fact when the signed grant arrives; nonces are single use and last 60 seconds. `osstack mission chain-decide --chain RC --decision abandon|takeover|reconcile` closes a live chain (voiding its reservation and asking for the running child to be cancelled) and needs no terminal; `reconcile` is reported as unavailable until the receipt reader lands. `osstack mission grant-keygen --key ID` creates the issuer key pair on the host. Migration 53 adds two triggers and one unique index (at most one pending push intent per chain): a live chain's grant may be replaced only upward and never rebound to another Mission, PR, repository or scope, and a shadow chain's grant is fixed. Core gains the grant schema and signing helpers, `RevisionChainStore.replaceGrant`/`stopLive`/`closeLive`/`transaction`, and a budget origin in `budget_json`; the CLI gains `assertGrantLive`/`enforceGrantLive` for the dispatch and push fences of later phases and a live-chain grant summary (no signature, digest, nonce or identities) in `mission status --json`. The grant RPC methods answer `bridge_live_disabled` and `OSSTACK_BRIDGE=1` is still refused with `BRIDGE_LIVE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`: nothing is dispatched or pushed yet. The upgrade gate now requires both grant triggers on schema 53 homes and refuses older homes that already carry them.
