Add bridge observability (phase 7 prerequisite). `osstack mission status --json` now carries the shadow chain's latest ten events in `chain.events` (kind, time and allow-listed fields only: fixed codes, object ids, counts and review ids; never bodies, logins or URLs) plus `chain.events_total` (counted up to 10 000, `events_total_capped` says when it was cut). `osstack stats` gains a `bridge` section computed read-only from the revision-chain tables on the daemon's connection and loaded lazily (no CLI startup cost): chains by mode and state, `awaiting_decision` reasons, shadow decisions by action (total and last 24h), observation-cycle and `read_failed` counts with a failure ratio and `read_failed` by code over the last 24h (scan capped at the newest 20 000 events, `window_truncated` says so), live reservation and intent counts, and two design §6 invariants (`rounds_over_grant`, `open_past_deadline`). Metrics that need phase 5 or data not yet recorded (cycle duration, revision round duration, changes-requested-to-push median, first-round pass rate, ledger/branch mismatch, aggregated discards, back-off state) are listed under `deferred` as null with a reason. The section is empty-safe and reports `available: false` when the tables do not exist.
