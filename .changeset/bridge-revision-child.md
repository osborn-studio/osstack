Add the same-PR revision child Mission for the Grok bridge (phase 5-B). A live chain can now reserve a round, create a child Mission on a new local branch `osstack/mission/<id>` starting at the chain's published head, and dispatch it in one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction (all PR reads, feedback extraction and grant, budget and protected-set checks happen before it, so a crash leaves exactly one child with its reservation, or nothing). The child inherits the root's goal, repository, allowed paths, caps, verify command, verification paths and publication terms field by field; `publish_target` and `revision_feedback` exist only on specs the dispatcher builds (`mission.start` cannot carry them). Reviewer text reaches the executor only inside a nonce-delimited block framed as untrusted data, redacted, stripped of control and bidi characters and bounded, and in repair turns as well; it cannot change allowed paths, verify or the protected set. A revision child's diff is also classified from the root's start commit, so a within-caps root cannot be pushed over its caps by revisions. Budget is charged per launch (`chargeLaunch`: executor turns and verifications), refunded down to actual time, held per child, and topped up on a budget re-grant by exactly the raise; `mission decide resume_budget|resume_grant` re-enters the launch that was stopped, and a halted Mission may re-enter `candidate_ready` only as the same candidate. `ports.publish` refuses any spec with a `publish_target`, and the runner stops a revision child at `delivering` before any publish port runs: nothing can be pushed or opened yet (the revision publisher is 5-C), and `OSSTACK_BRIDGE=1` is still refused.
