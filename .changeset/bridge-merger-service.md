Add the App E shadow merger service (M-C). With `OSSTACK_MERGER=shadow` and a host-private `$OSSTACK_HOME/bridge/merger.json`, the contained-Mission service observes `pr_opened` Mission PRs read-only (host `gh` credentials, GraphQL query documents and allowlisted `GET` paths only) and records what App E would do: per PR a fenced observation, the M-A gate verdict (`would_merge`, `refused`, `unevaluated` with fixed codes), a cycle row per scan, and the real outcome (`merged`, `closed_unmerged`, `reopened`, `observation_expired`), polled hourly for closed PRs and for 30 days. A scan runs every five minutes on its own timer (never on the Mission tick), at most ten PRs and 400 requests per scan, at least five minutes between two evaluations of a PR. The merger is built by its own factory from a read-only Mission view, the shadow writer, the read transports, the config and a clock; it never calls `loadBridgeConfig` and never reaches App credential code. `OSSTACK_MERGER` unset or `0` is off, `shadow` without `merger.json` is off with a log line, `1` or `live` fail the service start with `MERGER_LIVE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` and any other value with `MERGER_MODE_INVALID`. New `osstack mission merger report [--json]` (RPC `mission.merger_report`, read scope) classifies shadow decisions against real outcomes per the design table, SELECT-only, with no recommendation.
